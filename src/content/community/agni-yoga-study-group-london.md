The group meets regularly from 7:00 to 9:00 pm on the first Wednesday of each calendar month at the Hampstead Community Centre, 78 Hampstead High Street, London NW3 1RE. The nearest Tube station is Hampstead on the Northern line, a five-minute walk away.

The group works on a two-year recurring cycle, focusing each month on one of the Agni Yoga books in turn, in their published sequence, followed chronologically by the two volumes of Helena Roerich's published *Letters*, and then *At the Threshold of the New World* and Mme Roerich's two books, *On Eastern Crossroads* and *Foundations of Buddhism*.

Taking it in turns, every member present selects a paragraph, reads it out, and leads the discussion on it. There are short opening and closing meditations.

There is no formal membership and no dues payable. Those wishing to start attending meetings should first contact the Group Convener, Jean Archer, either by phone or email. A monthly email is sent on behalf of the convener to all members, giving details of the date, time, and agenda for that month's meeting.
