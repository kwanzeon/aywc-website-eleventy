Searching for a group in New York City? Not just to bury our faces in books but to share what we know and what we wish we knew about Agni Yoga—this fiery Yoga of the higher mind and beyond, as well as about the Masters and the Roerichs.

And no matter how like-minded we may be in our search to understand the deeper levels of esoteric knowledge, we could also spend time doing other things, such as going to movies or going on a hike, checking out the theater scene, or sharing a meal together.

We could meet whenever we want, on the web or in person, whatever works—whether frequently or infrequently, formal or informal.

My name is Thomas McDermott, and if you want to reach me, send a message using the form on this page.
