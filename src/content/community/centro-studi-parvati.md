Centro Studi Parvati, founded in 2010, is an Italian cultural and study center dedicated to the exploration and dissemination of spiritual, philosophical, and cultural teachings that foster the evolution of human consciousness. The Center is rooted in and founded upon the Agni Yoga Teaching.

Through study meetings, cultural initiatives, conferences, and opportunities for shared reflection, Centro Studi Parvati seeks to promote a deeper understanding of the principles of Living Ethics and their application in everyday life.

The Center values dialogue, Beauty, Culture, conscious development, and service as means of fostering both individual and collective growth.

Based in Turin, Italy, Centro Studi Parvati welcomes opportunities for exchange and collaboration with students, study groups, and organizations within the international Agni Yoga community.
