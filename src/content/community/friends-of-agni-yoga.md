We meet online, every two weeks on Friday from 6:30 to 8 PM. Participation is free of charge.

The group starts with a short meditation sending light and love all over the world and where the need is greatest. Quotes from the AY series are selected (not in advance) each time and are read twice. This is followed by a guided group discussion on the quote read, and as much as possible on more highly developed intuition.
